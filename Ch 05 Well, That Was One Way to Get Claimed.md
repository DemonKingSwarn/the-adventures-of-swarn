# Chapter 5: Well, That Was One Way to Get Claimed

Later that night, the hooded man with the scythe returned in my dreams. But this time, he had spoken to me. “You are going to be the key, little hero.” He said. 

“Who are you?” I screamed.

To which he just chuckled like I told him a joke. Ria had told me that dreams to demigods like us are kind of like a message or a glimpse of the future, so I wondered what this dream could mean. 

The next morning, we gathered up for Capture the Flag. Vajivega started speaking “The rules of Capture the Flag are, no killing or maiming, no going outside of the boundaries of the camp. Now start.” He blows into a sea snail’s giant shell to indicate it has begun. Don’t ask how I know whose shell it is, I just know for some reason. 

Sujan, Ria, and I are in the same team. Sujan took half the team with him, while I and Ria went together. 

“So, what do you think of the last night?” I asked.

“I have some theories, but I would need some time to be sure of it. I am like 90% sure right now.” Ria replied.

By seeing how she is so sure of it, I felt like I am the only one who doesn’t see the answer which was right in front of me all this time. 

…

She and I went to guard our flag, alone. I laid there on the rock while she strategised. 

She was guarding the flag while strategising our every move.

She then looked at me curiously and asked “Are you going to just lie there like we are on a picnic or help me?”

I chuckled.

But then Sanvi and her gang circled us out of no where and Sanvi walked forward with her spear. 

“Let’s see if you can handle this.” Sanvi said, mockingly.

“Isn’t it like “no maiming” one rule you can’t break?” I said.

“Oh I know, I might lose dessert privileges for some time.” Sanvi replied.

Sanvi lunged at me and I immediately uncapped my pen and used the trident to block her spear. Damn, she’s good, I guess it makes sense, she is the daughter of the war god. 

We exchanged blows, she landed another strike at me which Ria blocked using her dagger. And then I somehow made the ground below us shake and two of Sanvi's friends fell.

But eventually I had the same dancing sensation again, but this time I felt something stronger in my forehead.
And I suddenly became a lot more aggressive, and my body felt like it just got refuelled with energy. I lunged forward and easily broke Sanvi’s spear in half.

Sanvi screamed at me and was about to lunge forward. 

But before it could happen, the sound of shells can be heard again indicating the Capture the Flag has ended. 

But then I looked at Ria and she was looking above me. 

“What happened?” I asked her.

“Your dad’s calling!” Ria replied.

And I looked up, and a big holographic green trident was above me. And then I looked back at her, but then she and every other camper, including even Vajivega kneeled. 

“You have been claimed by Shiva, Nataraj, The Destroyer, Kaal Bhairav, Lord of Animals. Swarn, the son of Shiva.” Vajivega said. 

And suddenly I was not the unclaimed demigod living in the Narad’s cabin, I am Swarn, the son of Shiva.

“As the forbidden child of the destroyer god, you will stay in the Shiva cabin.” Vajivega added.

The moment I entered the Shiva cabin, I noticed how quite it was, there were literally no other demigods. Just like how I saw the Vishnu cabin, because his only daughter Astrid is now a peepal tree, and the Brahma Cabin. 

I turned to Vajivega and asked “Why is that all big three cabins and the Parvati cabin are empty?”

To which he replied with a little hesitancy “It is because, long ago Shiva, Vishnu, and Brahma had heard a prophecy that either Vishnu or Shiva’s child would be a problem in the future. Which is why they swore an oath to never sire any demigod children.” And the whole time he said it, he never once looked at me in the eyes. 

"You didn't told me why the Parvati cabin is empty?" I asked.

"It's because she doesn't have any demigod children, we offer her a cabin here at camp as respect." Vajivega replied.
