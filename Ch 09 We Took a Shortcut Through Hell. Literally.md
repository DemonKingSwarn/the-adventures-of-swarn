# Chapter 8: We Took a Shortcut Through Hell. Literally. 

We left the car at Uttar Pradesh, damn that thing is fast. I guess the god of travelers would have a fast car. We were inside that Narad's Abode for literal two days and it felt like minutes to us.

Ria left a note on the car's windshield with the keys, the note says "You can have it back now."

Now we need to find the entrance to Patal more than ever.

“So how will we know we found the entrance?” I asked.

“Well the entrance is probably where we will see a lot of souls forming a line to enter it.” Ria replied. “Maya will hide our mythical world from humans, so it can be right in front of our eyes because sometimes Maya even works on us demigods too.” She added.

I look at Aryan and he was already trying to flirt with some stranger.

“Don’t you have a girlfriend or something?” I said.

To which Aryan just shrugged.

“So will we find Diti and Danu in Patal too?” I asked Ria.

“Uhm I don’t think so, they aren’t dead after all, plus they aren’t the chthonic gods.” Ria replied.

…

We continued walking till we found a building which read “Yama’s Concert! Will start at 21:00”.

“This must be the entrance.” Ria said.

We looked at her and then proceeded to enter the building. And what we saw was thousands or even more dead souls waiting in some sort of waiting area while something is letting them pass one by one.

There was this man who looked like he has been there since 1990, then there was this woman who wore clothes from 1700s.

We approached the man at the counter.

“I smell the living.” He said. “Who are thee?” 

“Hello sir, uhm we need to get to the Patal.” I said nervously.

“Wait for your turn, young one. You aren’t dead yet.” He said.

“But we need to…” Ria said but then the man interrupted her.

“I smell the scent of demigods. I don’t like them in my office.” He said.

As he approached her to grab us, we ran through his door which led us straight to Patal without his permission.

There we were, in the realm of the Patal, where all the dead souls come but we weren’t scared. Okay we were a little scared. 

We might have passed that receptionist’s gate to enter here, but we still aren’t in the Patal. We need to cross the river Vaitarani to truly enter it.

I tried to use the winged shoes Sujan gave me to cross the Vaitarani river, but the river didn’t like it and started pulling me. Ria and Aryan managed to get hold of me just in time before I fell into the river. 

“Phew! That was close.” I said.

I crossed that idea to cross the river with this, and we walk forward towards the river and we see a ferryman taking them across. And there were few who tried to go across without his need and got stuck in the river eternally. 

“That’s Tarakeshwar, uhm your dad’s one of the forms who ferry the souls across.” Ria said.

We approached the ferryman and I moved forward.

“Uhm hi dad?” I said nervously. “Are you actually uhm his form?”

Tarakeshwar just looked at me but didn’t say anything.

“uhm we need to go across the river.” I said slowly.

Tarakeshwar just gestured us to get into his boat.

Once we were in, he started rowing. 

While we were in the boat, we saw that the waters of river Vaitarani has turned black and lots of toys, or watches, or other possessions are floating in it.

“The incomplete human desires have been polluting these waters. The moment they die, they started bringing their incomplete desires with them here.” Tarakeshwar said in a raspy voice. “The waters here used to be so clean that you could see the souls trapped in them from trying to cross it themselves. Now all you see is their guilt, their unfinished desires in the realm of the living.”

…

We approached the end of our journey. We got out of the boat and bowed to show our respect to Tarakeshwar as he turned his boat around and went back.

We were standing in the Pitriloka, the neutral zone for the souls who weren’t truly evil or heroic. For the ones who lived an average life, in terms of their deeds obviously. While the truly heroic beings go to Vaikuntha, the eternal paradise.

Picture this, a vast amount of trees with souls living in some sort of caves-like structures, and ash marks on the ground, and you would be able to picture the Pitriloka.

“I never thought I actually considered Patal to have a neutral zone and also the eternal paradise before. I always thought it was only Narak.” I said slowly, feeling a bit embarrassed by my knowledge of Patal.

“That’s okay, most people think the same too.” Ria replied softly.

That is when we encountered two four eyed hounds, they were growling at us.

“Those are Yama’s hounds, Sharvara and Shyama.” Ria mumbled slowly.

“What are you two doing here?” I said, I don’t know why but I feel like I can talk to them.

They woofed at us, at least I thought they did because I could hear them speaking to me.

“You ask us this question which we should be asking you?” Sharvara said.

Ria and Aryan looked at me with wide eyes because they thought I had lost my mind.

“How am I able to understand you guys?” I asked, just as intrigued as Ria and Aryan were.

“It is because of your connection to your father. He is the lord of animals, boy.” Shyama said. “Now get out of here, and come back when you are dead.” He added.

“Please?” I asked pleadingly.

Thats when Ria threw a rubber ball at a distance and both Shyama and Sharvara ran after it.

“Hurry guys, they won’t be distracted for long.” Ria said urgently.

So we ran towards the palace. We were here to just take the shortcut to Dandakaranya and now it’s kind of starting to feel like a really bad idea. 

The palace was grand, and made of black marble. Though surprisingly the garden was lush green, for a realm which contains the dead soul. Which I believe is probably because of Yama’s twin sister Yami, or most know her by the name Yamuna. 

We reach the heart of the palace where we found the big magnificent throne, and by the side of it stood a man with a book in his hand. 

We slowly approach the throne, that’s when Yama turned to face us in his throne and started walking down to us.

He wore a black suit which felt like they had thousands of souls trapped in them, his skin was as pale as a vampire’s. He had long messy black hair.

And then we got a clear look on the man on the side. He was chitragupt, and he wore a simple dress of a lawyer.

“So you finally found your way to me on your own accord.” Yama said. “I was about to send them again to you. The other one wasn’t this much of a problem like you are.”

“What do you mean?” Ria asked.

“Well let’s just say I sent one of my Yamaduttas to retrieve this boy, after all his father is also the one who broke the oath just like Vishnu. And I would be called biased if I don’t come for him too like I did for that girl.” Yama said.

And exactly that moment a bat-like humanoid creature flew at us and stood at the back rest of Yama’s throne. That’s when it clicked that she was Mr Shristi, the substitute teacher who attacked me at the museum.

“Nothing personal kid, it’s just they took the oath on my name. And I take these kinds of things very seriously.” Yama said.

Then I looked at Ria who by the looks of it she was getting angry, maybe because it was because of Yama which led to Astrid almost dying before Vishnu turned her to a tree.

And then I saw what’s on the other side of the throne, it was my mom in the gold form, frozen. My breath hitched.

“Oh you noticed? I sent that Mahishasura to fetch your mom for me, as leverage to get you here.” Yama said. “I can let her go, if you leave yourself to me.”

“Will you let her go if we help finding out what is wrong with Dandakaranya?” I asked quietly.


“Oh a deal? Well I have been known for making deals. I can accept that, but I wonder how will you escape my realm, hm? Even other gods have to obey me.” Yama said. "Well my armour was stolen from me, if you can fetch it then sure go ahead, I want to see you try." He added.

“We accept this deal.” I said.

“Well then, but let’s see how you can get out of this realm without my permission, because no god is coming to help you here.” Yama said.

But then I had an Idea, and I closed my eyes and started praying to Diti. He said no god dares to interfere in the matters of his realm, but surely she can.

I said in my mind “uhm hello? It’s me, can you uhm get me and my friends out of Patal?” 

I opened my eyes and hoped Diti got my prayer.

And then in a flash of light we were out of Patal, lying on the forest floor.
