# Chapter 1: I Think I Just Imagined Killing a Monster

I woke up from my power nap in the bus, I feel like I slept for hours. I look around and notice everyone was moving down. Today we are at a field trip at a museum about Hindu Mythology, one of the only topics I like for some reason. 

Oh you don’t know me yet, I am Swarn, I am twelve years old and I am a troubled kid. I have like switched 3 schools in 3 years. And I got a step-dad at home who is an asshole, I feel bad for my mom who has to live with him alone while I am at the dorm of this Pallavi High. I have got both dyslexia and ADHD, yeah I am weird like that.
Ever since I was a kid, I see things which most people couldn't. Like that one time there was this flying horse on the roof of a building opposite to my class'.

I was standing with other kids in my class group in front of a statue of a Hindu hero. And that's when Alison Hogan, the class bully threw a paper ball at me which hit my head. She and her friends started laughing.

"That's enough!" Mr Sharma said as he moved his wheel chair towards me.

Mr Sharma looked at me empathically and said “Swarn, will you like to join us in the class? Tell me who this hero is in this statue.”

I looked at the statue and then the Sanskrit carvings on it, for some reason I can easily understand Sanskrit. I focused on it and it read “Parshuram”, and then I told him confidently “It’s Parshuram.”

“Correct!” said Mr Sharma, “I knew you could do it.” He added.

He then probably noticed that I lost my pen because he came closer to me and gave me one of his cool-looking ball point pen. 
“Take care of it, it’s a mighty weapon.” Mr Sharma said. 

…

It was the lunch break and we all got sandwiches, I was sitting with my only friend here, who is also my best friend. His name is Aryan, and he is also the bottom of the food chain like me. Also he kinda have this leg injury which makes him uncomfortable to walk or sit with us, and he always wears full pants to hide his legs. 

We were talking and having fun when Alison Hogan, threw a cheese slice in Aryan’s hair. He is always scared of bullies, and he said slowly "Ignore her. One thing I know about bullies is you should never fight them back."

I looked at him like he has gone mad and said "Seriously? That is the least thing you should do."

So I stood up for him and as soon as I reached for her, something happened and I blacked out. But for a brief moment of consciousness, I can feel cool water. 

When I opened my eyes, I can see Alison Hogan was in the fountain, but last I remembered I never even got to touch her to even push her to the fountain. 
And she started screaming “Swarn hit me, he pushed me into this fountain.”

And suddenly I can feel someone grab my hand, it was Mrs. Shristi, our substitute teacher who always hated me and loved her oh so precious Alison Hogan. So I knew I was for sure in trouble now. She dragged me out of the group while Aryan looked at me with panicked eyes. 

She took me to a secluded area in the museum. 

“Hey, uhm Mrs Shristi, I didn’t push her into that fountain.” I said pleadingly. 

“You thought we couldn’t find you? That we wouldn’t know who your father is?” She said. 

It made me confused and I replied “What about him? Is it about my step-dad? Did he call or something?”

“Oh you little thing, don’t you play with me” She said, and as she said, her body turned fully black with leathery bat wings coming out of her and then she lunged at me. 
I was on the floor with her on top of me, but before she could hurt me, the pen Mr Sharma gave me turned to something big like a staff with three thongs or something and last I remembered she was turned to dust. 

I stood up and looked at my hand and the pen was surprise-surprise, a pen. “uhm what just happened here? Is anyone here? Mrs Shristi?” I said. 

I put it all on my ADHD, as it always makes me imagine some things which actually feel real. I went out of the secluded area and noticed Alison Hogan was still drenched with some teacher I never saw as holding a towel to her. 

I approached Aryan and asked him “uhm hey dude, where is Mrs Shristi?”, he looked at me like I said something really funny and then replied “Who?”
