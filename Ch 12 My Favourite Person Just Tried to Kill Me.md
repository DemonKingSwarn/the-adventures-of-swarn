# Chapter 12: My Favourite Person Just Tried to Kill Me

Later that night, when everyone was celebrating. Sujan took me for a little walk to where we always trained.

“I heard you fought Kartikeya during your quest.” Sujan said.

“Yeah, it was more of an instinct.” I replied. “I did rely a lot on your training.”

“There’s one more thing, uhm I lost the shoes you gave me when I was at Mansarovar. They acted  like they had the minds of their own and started dragging me towards the lake.” I said slowly, hesitantly.

Though Sujan didn’t look surprised at all, he looked like he was expecting that to happen.

“Wait! The prophecy said…” I hesitated the bit.

“You shall go where no demigod has returned from” I spoke softly.

“Your journey to Mansarovar.” Sujan replied.

“You shall face the god who has been turned.” I spoke again.

“Kartikeya” Sujan replied.

“Fail to save what matters most.” I said.

“It could only mean my mother at Yama’s court whom I had to leave behind at that time.”

Sujan nodded.

“And you shall be betrayed by the one you trust the most.” I said.

“It was you!” I said with sadness in my voice as I finally pieced together everything.

“I wasn’t expecting you to get out of those shoes.” Sujan said. “You could have joined my cause.”

“Your cause? What are you talking about?” I said.

“The gods have ruled for far too long, they have the ego at the size of a mountain. That’s why I joined Kāl’s forces, to overthrow them.” Sujan replied.

Suddenly I felt the same coldness I always felt whenever that hooded man spoke to me. That’s whom I was hearing from all this time, Kāl.

The scary part was I actually believed him. Though if I hadn't met my father, I might have actually joined his side.

I took out my ballpoint pen again and uncapped it. And Sujan took out his sword. It was a different blade, not made from celestial bronze which we demigods use for our weapons. Half of it was celestial bronze and the other half was normal human steel.

Sujan was quick to attack, but I managed to block it in the last moment.

And when I lunged forward with my trident, a scorpion beat me. It wasn’t the scorpion I recognised though. I fell on the ground.

“Don’t try it! It’s from Narak, no mortal has the cure for its venom.” Sujan said.

“Farewell, Swarn.” Sujan said.

And then he sliced his sword in the air and a rift opened through it and he went through and the rift was gone.

I struggled, but eventually started crawling towards the big house. I fell unconscious when I reached the foothills of Astrid’s peepal tree.

…

I felt someone feeding me.

I slowly opened my eyes and I see Ria feeding me Amrit and Somras.

“You are awake.” Ria said, softly. “We found you unconscious.”

I told her, Varuni, and Vajivega exactly what happened back there, and Sujan’s betrayal. It broke Ria, after all she came to camp with Sujan when she was 7 years old. It made me a lot angry.

…

The next day, we got this summer’s bead for our bead necklaces, which makes it my first bead. This year it had the symbol of a trident, marking my coming to the camp.


Ria, me, and Aryan were standing at the Astrid’s peepal tree. Planning to leave for the outside world, and I was excited to see my mother again.

Ria decided to go home for the first time in 5 years, since she came.

“Let’s promise to meet here next year.” I said.

“Yeah.” Ria said, smiling.

“We could be the Three Musketeers.” Aryan said.

To which all three of us laughed. And then we hugged each other and finally left camp.

…

When I reached back my apartment at Bangalore, where we stay. I stopped at my tracks, as I just remembered by idiot step-dad.

But then right outside of our door I found a note.

“I told you I keep my side of the deal. Your mom is free, including from your step-dad. He is in my realm now, awaiting judgement from me.” 

And it was signed by Yama.

I sighed with relief and then entered through the door. I immediately went to find my mom and just hugged her.
