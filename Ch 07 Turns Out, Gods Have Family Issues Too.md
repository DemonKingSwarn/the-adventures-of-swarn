# Chapter 7: Turns Out, Gods Have Family Issues Too

So after that Aryan, Ria, and I said goodbye to everyone and then left camp for our quest. We also got some Somras and Amrit in our flasks for when we are injured. 

We are mostly going to be walking on foot, till we at least reach the railway station anyways. 

"So what you guys think our quest is about?" I asked.

"Don't know." Aryan said.

"I guess we will see it when we reach our destination. It's my first time on a quest too." Ria said, softly.

That's when Aryan started smelling someone making food, here out of nowhere.

"Hey dude where are you going?" I asked, shouting a little.

Ria and I started going after him, as he lead us to a small house but cosy looking house.

"Aryan, this is not in our quest's path." I said. "We should go."

Aryan ignored us and knocked on the door.

A beautiful young woman opened the door, and she smiled at us softly.

"Oh, kids, what are you doing here? Where are your parents?" She asked.

"Uhm we were just travelling when I got hungry and smelled the food you were making." Aryan replied.

"Oh? Come on in, it's been a while since I had guests around here." She said.

Aryan went inside, and I looked at Ria to know what to do. But we eventually gave in and went inside too.
We sat at the dining table while she served us food.

"So what are you kids doing out here alone?" She asked. "It's not always I get demigods at my door." She added.

It made Aryan choke on his food he was already started to munch on.

"How do you know we were demigods?" Ria asked. "Are you someone from our world?" She added.

"Am I someone from your world? Oh sweetheart, I am someone who have witnessed the cruelty and double nature of the gods. I am Ahalya." She replied.

"Wait, weren't you punished by Sage Gautam for cheating on him?" I asked.

"So that's what the gods have twisted my story to become now? I never cheated on him, it was Indra, that super egoistic king of the gods who one day when my husband was out came as him. And instead of punishing Indra, he cursed me to become an unseen stone for so long." She replied, a little angrily. "Not all gods are who you think they are. They toy with you and you have to bare the punishment."

I looked at Ria and Aryan.

"I know what Indra did to you was wrong, but you can't put his mistake on other gods and on us." Ria said, softly.

"I know the gods aren't perfect, I am not disagreeing with you here." I replied. "Infact, I think they should have stood up for you and punished Indra for his mistake."

"Oh you sweet kids. If the kids of those gods are like this, maybe there is still hope in this world." She said. 

"You know, ever since I was freed from my curse, I distanced myself from my husband whom I once loved dearly." Ahalya replied. "You kids have nothing to fear from me. Though it's a good thing you knocked on my door when you were hungry rather than some mortal's doors. They would have worried over you so much."

I looked at Ahalya with a little guilt because I didn't mean to come out as rude.

Ahalya looked at me as if she understood why I looked guilty and said "Oh? Please don't be guilty, you were just scared."

After that conversation, we just sat there for we don't know how long. We thought it would be too disrespectful to just leave after hearing her story. We drank some masala chai with her too.

After that we finally started getting ready to leave and walked out.

"Have a good journey to wherever you are going." Ahalya said and closed her doors.

While we were walking away, Ria nudged me and said "You spoke like Astrid back there. She would have said the exact same thing if she was here."

I didn't knew how to respond to that so I just chuckled.

...

After that unexpected encounter, we finally reached the train station and got the tickets. We entered our train booth and sat down.
The train booth was small but it was alright and kind of safe.

I looked at Ria and Aryan.

Aryan started singing to lighten the mood. Both Ria and I looked confused to this but laughed together.

Later that night in the train, Aryan was sleeping soundly. I was lying on the seat below Ria’s.
I couldn’t sleep. 

“Are you awake too?” I asked to see if Ria couldn’t sleep as well. 

“Yeah, it’s my first time outside of camp since I first came when I was 7.” Ria replied.

“How can we reach Dandakaranya in just three days though?” I asked curiously, trying to change the topic.

“We can take a little detour from this path and take a shortcut through Patal.” Ria pondered.

“Through Patal?” I asked. “Isn’t it like where all evil souls go?” 

“That’s not exactly true, Patal is where all dead souls go. No matter if you are evil or not, that’s where you get judged on your deeds and then your punishment is decided. Souls never go to Swarg as the modern media suggests.” Ria replied.

“Patal has layers in itself, Narak is one of them where the wicked go.” Ria added.

“Have you ever missed your life outside of camp?” I asked.

“No. My father re-married, with my step-mother he had three kids. And she always hated me, so I ran away. That’s when I met Sujan and Astrid while I was on the road.” Ria replied, slowly with hesitancy. 

“How was Astrid, when you met her?” I asked.

“She was a lot like you actually.” Ria replied, slowly. “Just like you she was extremely loyal to the people she cared for, and just like you she wasn’t shy to disobey the gods.” Ria added.

We hear Aryan mumbling in his sleep “I want more bhaang.”

Both Ria and I started laughing at that.

…

The next morning when we were just sitting in our compartment, discussing everything while having breakfast, we heard a little scratch on the window.
When we looked outside, we couldn’t see anything which could have scratched it.

Then we heard a knock on our cabin door, and when we opened it there were two beautiful women standing. 

“Hello kids, did you lose your parents?” The first woman who looked much older than the other spoke. 

“No, mam. We are just travelling, no parents allowed. Though they are watching.” I said, though the second part isn’t a lie, I mean we are children of the gods, they are watching. 

“Oh I am sure they are watching. I am Danu by the way, and this is my sister Diti.” Danu said.

And then Ria looked like she connected few dots and said “Wait do you mean Danu and Diti as in the ones who gave birth to all the Danavas and Daityas? That Danu and Diti?” 

This made them smirk and then Diti said “You children are clever. Though we don’t mean you harm for now, unlike your parents who would harm our children without even thinking.”

“What do you mean? the gods kill your children because they create chaos.” Ria replied quickly. 

To which Danu scoffed and said “So that’s what they tell you, that our children are the enabler? Don’t they tell you that it’s always your parents who provoke our children, that whenever your king of the gods Indra is scared, he goes to that Vishnu who then tricks our children into doing something?”

“But doesn’t my father Shiva always take your children’s side? Shouldn’t that count for something?” I quickly replied.

To which Diti politely says, “Well Shiva has always been the only god even we respected because he respected us.”

“Respect or not, but he still never stops the other gods from manipulating our children. It pains us as a mother to them to see their downfall.” Danu said with distaste. 

“You are different from your half-brother Kartikeya, he would have killed us right away for saying how our children were wronged.” Diti says softly.

“I mean he is the god of war, and don’t call him my half-brother, I don’t even know him.” I said.

“Well don’t we all come from the same being the one known as The Nasadiya Sukta, the primordial chaos? It has always been a family feud.” Diti said.

“The gods are nothing like you asuras.” Ria said with a sharp voice.

“Foolish child, still supporting the gods when they would abandon you in a heartbeat when you need them the most.” Danu said. 

“Just like our own sister Aditi, the mother of your beloved gods had abandoned us.” Danu added with a scoff.

Diti says politely “We always loved our little sister Aditi, but she left us the moment she and her children got the power.”

And that’s when I had a little glimpse of that same hooded man with a scythe. But when I came out of my trance, I noticed that both Danu and Diti had noticed it too which kind of made them scared as if they recognised who that man is.

“What was that?” Diti asked with a little frightened voice while Danu remained calm but still surprised.

Ria and Aryan both exchanged looks.

“I don’t know, He has been in my dreams until now.” I said politely to Diti.

Something in Ria clicked and she asked “Describe to me how he appeared. Did he wear a black hood and have a scythe?”

I was surprised she guessed something from my dreams so correctly and then nodded and asked “How did you know?”

“Astrid used to tell me and Sujan about a recurring dream she had while we were on the road.” Ria replied.

Diti immediately moved forward to gently stroke my hair. I think she thought I needed a motherly comfort or something. But I didn’t push her away.

“You are always welcome to come to me if you ever want a mother.” Diti said softly just like a mother would to her own child. 

Seeing how Diti reacted to my vision that scared them makes me believe that the gods aren’t exactly what they seem like. Maybe we were taught about them only the version they want us to know.

But one thing which makes me wonder now is what this mysterious man wants with both me and Astrid?

For the first time I heard Danu speaking softly to us, or me “You have been intertwined in another one of their games. Just so you know, we were never here to hurt you. We are only here to have what has been stolen from us, and we fear you have it.” 

“What has been stolen from you?” I asked.

Diti while still comforting me said “It’s one of our dead children’s blades. In wrong hands they are far more devastating than it was in his hands. And we don’t want the gods who killed him to have it either. It’s a blade made of bones.”

It made me remember that specific blade I saw at the camp attic, and I replied “Oh I think I saw it at the camp attic. I promise you we will return it to you if you let us complete this quest.”

Both Danu and Diti looked at each other, but when Danu was about to say something, Diti stopped her and said “We believe you.” While she was still stroking my hair.

It’s scary how much I am starting to trust them, mostly Diti.

Diti slowly kissed my forehead and whispered “You are strong, don’t let anyone including even our children cloud your judgement. Soon you will need to make an important choice if he is truly the one speaking to you.”

“You will have my blessing when you need it, even if my sister here won’t give you her’s.” Diti said with a little bit of playfulness.

“Your blessing? Like when we are in dire need of something, you will help us?” Ria asked hesitantly.

To which Diti nodded and said goodbye to us and grabbed her sister Danu and left.
