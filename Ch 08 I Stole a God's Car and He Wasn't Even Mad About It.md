# Chapter 8: I Stole a God's Car and He Wasn't Even Mad About It

Our train stopped at Madhya Pradesh, so we had to again continue our journey on foot as even the Patal shortcut was a lot further from here.

"So where to now?" I asked.

"Well we are still very far from our destination, even Patal." Ria replied.

That's when we saw a weird building and it read "Narad's Abode".

"Do you guys think it's the actual god Narad or something they just named it after him?" I asked.

"Who knows? It can be either." Aryan replied.

We went inside the building and it had so many people living inside like a community, though something felt off.

"Guys, stay close. I don't feel good about this place." Ria said.

And before I could say anything to her, we notice Aryan was already gone. We started looking for him.

"Where do you thing a stupid bhuta can go?" I asked.

"Don't know, but can you believe it? He went right when I said I didn't feel good about this place." Ria replied.

But then Ria has also disappeared.

...

I asked a guy who was reading a newspaper, "Hey, did you happen to see a girl who was with me a few moments ago?"

"Nope." He replied, but his gestured felt weird. Like too formal like the ones I see on those tv shows based on ancient times.

"Uhm what year is this?" I asked him.

"1740, why?" He replied.

Which surprised me because he should have been dead by now.

Something tells me that this is no ordinary place. I walked a little further where I found a middle aged man who was acting surprisingly normal for this kind of place, but still being weird.

I approached him.

"uhm Hello?" I said.

"Oh? Oh it's you kiddo, the one who is quite popular amongst us recently. It's good to finally meet you Swarn, I am Narad." He said, smiling like a weirdo.

"What are you doing here? And what is this place?" I asked.

"Well I am having fun, besides don't you know my story with the illusions of Maya? After that I did start using it for my advantage, keeping few mortals in here for centuries to have fun with me. To them, they are still living in their time." He replied.

"That's cruel!" I said.

"Cruel? Since when having fun is cruel?" He replied.

At that moment I kinda saw why Sujan would hate his father.

"Where are my friends?" I asked, but unlike my earlier polite question, I was angry.

"Oh? You want them back? Sure, but they were having fun before this, the Maya probably gave them some adventure." He said, and then snapped his fingers and they appeared in front of us.

"mhm" Aryan said.

"Huh? What just happened?" said Ria.

I immediately hugged them and then faced Narad with them.

"You kids are no fun, I should throw you all out right now!" Narad said.

"We are not fun? You are the one who kidnapped everyone here. Also Sujan was right, you suck." I retorted, which I think made him a bit sad at the mention of Sujan.

"Do not bring him up!" Narad demanded.

"Or what? You would do the same thing you did with his mom to us?" Ria replied, angrily.

And then Narad looked at her and realised something and said "I remember you, you were there that night, with that daughter of Vishnu. When I visited Sujan, on the streets."

I then see Aryan took something from Narad's pockets without him noticing, and I grabbed both Aryan and Ria by their hands so this time we wont lose each other and ran.

I managed to drag them with me, but because I wasn't able to find the door we entered through, I took the stairs and we entered the car parking or something like that.

Then Aryan showed us what he took from Narad, some sort of car keys.

I took the keys from Aryan's hand and pressed the "open doors" button, and ran towards the sound.

Ria found the note on the windshield and took it out and read "Clever, stealing from god of thieves, messenger, and travelers."

He knew we stole from him, but I didn't care.

We got inside the Narad's car, and then as I am the only one who kind of knows better of the mortal world, I sat on the driving seat.Though everyone was panicking for some reason.

I started the car, and accelerated forward. I bumped into the wall while taking it out of the building.
