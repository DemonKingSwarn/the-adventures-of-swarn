# Chapter 6: I Have a Prophecy. This Can't Be Good.

The interior of the cabin was made with dark blue marbles, with shivling at the
center of the cabin.

It felt lonely, I got used to the overcrowded Narad cabin, with everyone barely
getting a place to sit down. The shivling has some Sanskrit carvings on it.

Sujan and Ria visited me from time to time during the day breaks. 

Later that night I couldn’t sleep, I was lying on my bed, staring at the
ceiling.

That’s when I walked out of the cabin to give Astrid a visit. I sat down her
tree.

“So we both were never supposed to be born.” I said.

“Did you know about this?” I asked softly. “I guess, if they already knew you
were Vishnu’s daughter.” I added.

“Sujan told me you never liked your father Vishnu.” I said, slowly.

I just sat there under her for a long moment. I noticed Aryan coming at me.

“Hey, you couldn’t sleep too?” I said. 

“No, we Bhutas rarely sleep. I came to see you. Thought you might need a friend after that entire claiming ordeal.” Aryan said.

"Thanks, buddy. I was just kindof talking to Astrid. I mean you know, we both being the forbidden child and all. Though it's onesided." I said, slowly.

“I know. I was the one who accompanied Sujan, Ria, and Astrid to camp. I failed my duty when I let Astrid die, well technically she got turned to this tree. But you get what I mean.” Aryan added.

“Wait they came to camp with you? Like I did?” I asked.

Aryan nods.

"Anyway, I will see you soon. I have something to do tonight." Aryan said and left.

I leaned back against Astrid's tree again and looked up at the leaves and branches. 

"I wish you were here with me." I said, softly.

...

That night, I dreamed of the hooded man again. He has started to become a constant at this point. 

“So now you know who your father is, little hero.” He said in a raspy voice. “You have been lied to, by the gods. You are born to do so much more.” He added.

“Who are you?” I asked him again, with force this time.

To which he chuckled again and just left. But this time before he left, I caught a glimpse of gold in his scythe. 

…

The next morning I was called to the big house in front of both Vajivega and Varuni this time. They explained that the spirit of Akashvani wants to speak to me.

I enter the attic of the big house.

The attic was filled with a lot of things, and was a lot dusty as if it was never cleaned. There was also my Mahishasura horn there. The weirdest thing I saw there was this weird-looking blade made out of bones. 

Then my gaze dropped to a mummified body. Which I know is the Akashvani because of what they explained me.

I bowed respectfully and said “Hey, I am Swarn. I have been told that you wanted to speak with me.”

Nothing happened.

Then I said nervously “I think you are busy, I will come later.” And I turned around. 

But then I hear it standing up, with bones creaking. 
I turned to face it, and the green smoke started to come out of its mouth.
And then I saw a holographic image of someone I hate, my step-dad. 

“You shall go where no demigod has returned from, 
You shall face the god who has been turned.
Fail to save what matters most,
And you shall be betrayed by the one you trust the most.” It said in a raspy voice.

As soon as the green smoke and the image faded and the body fell back down on the chair, I rushed outside the attic.

…

I told Vajivega and Varuni the entire prophecy verbatim. Which made them wonder too. 

“Gather the camp.” Said Vajivega. “It’s time to announce a quest.” He added.

We all went to the Amphitheatre with me, Vajivega, and Varuni standing at the center of it.

“The quest will lead you to Dandakaranya. And you will face whatever evils you have to face.” Vajivega said.

“You may choose two who will accompany you in this quest.” said Varuni.

I looked at all the campers, and the words of the prophecy were echoing in my mind “You shall be betrayed by the one you trust the most.”

“I choose Ria and Aryan to go with me.” I said.

…

I sit at the edge of the shivling in my Shiva cabin while packing my bag. That’s when Sujan came to visit me.

I looked at Sujan and said "Uhm sorry about not choosing you. It's just the Akashvani said..."

"It's okay, I am not mad about it." Sujan replied. 
"Looks like Vajivega gave you quite a lot to pack." He added.

"Oh, he gave me some money and I don't know what are these, some kind of coin I guess." I said.

"Those are Nishka, money for the human world, and Nishka for the mythical world." Sujan replied.

"Here take these." Sujan said.

He handed me a box and said “This should come in handy during your quest.”

I opened it and saw a pair of shoes with wings forming from it’s shoelaces.

“They are something.” I said.

“It’s from my father.” Sujan replied. “I don’t need those.” He added. 

To what I have learned from Sujan so far is that even he doesn’t like his father much so I didn’t question it.

I walk out with him and notice Sanvi and other campers eyeing me. 

“Why are they looking at me?” I asked Sujan.

“Having a quest from the gods means glory.” Sujan replied. 

“So they are jealous?” I asked.

“Yeah, you can say that.” Sujan replied.
